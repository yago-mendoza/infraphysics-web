# No tics

One paragraph, pasted into every prompt that writes anything here (articles, tweets, wikinotes, UI copy) and applied by any agent working in this repo before it shows a text. It is not style: it is the residue a model leaves and the author does not want to find, in either language. The long form for articles is `src/data/pages/STYLE.md` (rules 1, 3, 9, 11); this is the version short enough to sit in every prompt.

Remove these from anything you write here before showing it, in English or Spanish: dashes as pauses (em-dash, en dash, double hyphen: a parenthesis, a period or a comma instead, chosen per phrase); the *not X, Y* reframe (*it's not about speed, it's about trust*: say the thing); the mic-drop line after a wind-up (*That's it.*); section selling (*here's where it gets interesting*); metacommentary on your own posture (*let me be direct*, *to be honest*); performed casualness (*vibes*, *rent-free*, *let that sink in*); the tricolon for gravitas; throat-clearing (*it's worth noting*, *furthermore*, *in summary*, *ultimately*); the hedged ending; decorated numbers (*an impressive 23%*); double quotes around a term (italics); arrows between concepts. When one appears, rebuild the sentence around what it was trying to say; never swap one tic for another, and never remove specificity to add polish.
