It describes a model linking automation, capital accumulation, redistribution and repression, with outcomes dependent on its assumptions and conditions, including the strength of revolt risk. It is not an unconditional empirical proof that AI will destroy democracy.

Possible essay question, proposed during capture: how does the political bargain change when owners of automated capital depend less on human labour?
