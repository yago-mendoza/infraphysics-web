# Adapting to AI: Relational Thinking and Artistic Cognition

Adapting to AI is not a question of using or not using it. It's a question of learning how to think differently.

The most important change relates to our sensemaking capabilities. The introduction of AI suddenly exposes something that artists already know intuitively: how magnificently and mysteriously interrelated are information systems, knowledge frameworks, and sensorial data.

**What becomes more important is the relation between various pieces of information, not the bits themselves.**

We are expanding from linear and analytical thinking into **topographical thinking**—a geography of thought. Artists know this without naming it. They read between the lines, listen between the sounds, look between the frames. What matters is how these things connect, how the specific combination creates an effect.

**The mind that will thrive in the era of AI is the mind capable of making sense of reality rhythmically and relationally. That is the mind of an artist.**

This is why it's so important to engage in:
- Artistic practices
- Creative research
- Everything that relates to your ability to make sense of data that is reality
