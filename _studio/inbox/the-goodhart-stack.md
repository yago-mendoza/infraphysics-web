Reward hacking runs on four layers at once. Biological: dopamine optimises sugar, not nutrition. Individual: you optimise credentials, not learning. Institutional: companies optimise metrics, not outcomes (watch time produces radicalisation). Artificial: RLHF teaches models to look aligned, not to be aligned. The pattern is identical because it is what optimisation does when the proxy diverges from the target. The alignment problem of AI is the human alignment problem, formalised.
