Naval: the demand for intelligence is infinite, there is no ceiling on how much compute the world wants. Musk's reply: when the physical loop closes (solar makes electricity, robots make things, those robots make chips, the chips run AI, the AI designs better robots and chips and energy) conventional money stops being what matters; what matters is watts and tons. The final bottleneck is not financial capital but physical infrastructure: energy and manufacturing. Connects directly to *we cannot cheat thermodynamics* and *the rate-limiting step is atoms*.

Also in this pile: *those who lived in software land are about to have a hard lesson in hardware*, which is the gap the author's profile covers.
