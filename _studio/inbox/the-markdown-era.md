More documentation, prompts and natural language than ever: Claude's official reports via /insights, skill documents, hooks, CLAUDE.md, writing style guides, TODOs, READMEs, and that is one vendor's environment (Gemini, Antigravity, Codex on top). The best proxy for how much AI is amplifying your output is the number of markdown files you ship in a day (diminishing returns apply). A second observation to fold in: letting an agent think for eight hours versus working with it in small steps, always working; the second means less checking.
