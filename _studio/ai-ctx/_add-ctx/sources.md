# Sources

Favourite places to look things up, for an article or a tweet alike: paste after a pack when the job needs facts, quotes or transcripts, or read before searching. One line per source, what it is good for, tags in brackets so `npm run find -- --tvb <text>` reaches it.

- **Hacker News** (news.ycombinator.com): what engineers are arguing about this week, and the comment threads under any paper or launch; search with hn.algolia.com. [discussion, engineering, en]
- **gwern.net**: long essays with sources on ML, scaling, spaced repetition and forecasting; the footnotes are the point. [essays, ml, scaling, en]
- **Dwarkesh Podcast** (dwarkesh.com): long interviews with the people building this, with transcripts; the source for what Ilya, Dario or Sholto said and when. [interviews, transcripts, agi, en]
