# thread-from-article

> Paste first, in order: `_format_ctx/ACCOUNT.md`, `_format_ctx/NO-TICS.md`, `../STRATEGY.md`, this file, `../queue/`. Optional after them: `_format_ctx/expressions-*.md`, `sentences.md`, `moves.md`, `avoid.md`, `_format_ctx/examples/`.

**Input:** one article of the site (pasted after this), or a cluster of wikinotes.

**Output:** one thread of eight to twelve tweets that works on its own (its own opening and its own landing, never a summary of the piece), one idea per tweet, the first tweet carrying the claim, the last linking the piece without selling it, in the article's language. Under it, one line per tweet saying which image or figure from the article would go with it, if any.

Then the variations, which for a thread are not three whole threads: **three different first tweets and three different spines**, each stated in two lines (what the thread would argue and in what order), so the author can pick the angle before the twelve tweets are written. Write the full thread for the one you would send, sketch the other two. Under all three: the profile test on the one you wrote out.

The three spines vary by **where the thread enters the material**: the claim first, the failure first (what went wrong and why it matters), or the number first. An article usually supports all three; they land on different readers.

Check the queue before writing: a piece already sitting there may be the right opening tweet, or may belong inside the thread. Say which, and do not repeat it in a new wording.
