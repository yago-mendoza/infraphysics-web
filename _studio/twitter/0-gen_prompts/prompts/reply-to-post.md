# reply-to-post

> Paste first, in order: `_format_ctx/ACCOUNT.md`, `_format_ctx/NO-TICS.md`, `../STRATEGY.md`, this file, `../queue/`. Optional after them: `_format_ctx/expressions-*.md`, `sentences.md`, `moves.md`, `avoid.md`, `_format_ctx/examples/`.

**Input:** someone else's post (text, author, link, and the replies already there if they are pasted), plus, optionally, the angle the author has in mind.

**Output:** three replies that add something to that conversation (a datum, a contradiction, an observation, an image idea), in the language of the post. Under each one: what it adds that was not already there. Under all three: to whom the reply is addressed (the post's author, or a specific reply worth answering instead, with the handle), which of the three you would send, and the profile test on that one.

Build each reply as the strategy's *The reply* section says: the ideal third participant, concede the frame then extend it, one observation from experience, one precise term and ordinary words around it, humour from register only. Roughly 70% extending the thesis, 30% friction. Take the premise further than its author did, to the uncomfortable place it was not mentioning, and stop before explaining.

The three vary by **what they bring to the table**, not by wording: one that adds the missing variable, one that concedes and then contradicts with something concrete, one that compresses the whole thing into an image or a line. If the queue already holds a piece that answers this post better than anything you would write, say so and quote it instead of inventing a fourth.

Kills for this job, on top of the standing ones: student questions, interview questions, lists of controls, anything that says *look, I know this*, going against the premise outright, trying to be funny.
