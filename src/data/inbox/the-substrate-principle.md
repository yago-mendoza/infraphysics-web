Queueing theory predicts TCP/IP, blood vessels, production plants and GPU pipelines with the same equations (Little's law). Flow control, buffering, load balancing and redundancy are the same problems whether you route packets or train a transformer. An engineer who understands systems does not need to *learn AI*; they need to learn where the same patterns appear in a new substrate. The technical barrier to ML is not the mathematics, it is the vocabulary.

This one is the author's own story more than a framework (*I'm an industrial engineer; here is what nobody tells you about learning ML from outside CS*). It could also be a prominent section of /about.
