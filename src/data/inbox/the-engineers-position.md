Three things are true at once and most people can hold only one: the technology works, the timeline is fantasy, the business models are speculative. Engineers do not predict, they measure. The transformer is a real advance in information processing and the scaling laws are empirically validated; half of venture capital going to AI in one year is allocation distortion, not signal. The useful question is not *will AI change everything* (yes) nor *when* (unknowable) but *what can I build with what exists today, and what breaks if I am wrong*.

*Everybody has an opinion on AI* is the nearest existing piece; this would be the explicit version.
