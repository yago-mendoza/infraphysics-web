Three pieces summarised in the raw notes, all about how AI reshapes engineering roles. Suster on professional services in enterprise startups: PS as an accelerator of software growth, never the business (tightly coupled to the product, IP stays with the startup, 15 to 25% of revenue at scale, sales paid less on PS than on software). A ground-level week of a forward-deployed engineer at an AI startup: ten clients, fifty hours, half meetings, full ownership with no handoff, the differentiator is pattern recognition across chaotic organisations. And the revival of the true full-stack developer through agent-assisted coding: speed becomes the baseline rather than a path to pay, the FDE risks becoming the old 10x fantasy rebranded, instability creates opportunity and exploitation, adaptability is now essential.
