The **Human Machine Interface** is what the operator uses: process diagrams, buttons, alarms, trends, temperatures, motor states.
- It can be a local screen on a machine, delivered by the OEM with the machine's own [[mfLejTTj|PLC]], or a set of screens inside a [[es6Sv4Zb|SCADA]] or [[7NrcWZUv|DCS]].
- What the operator can do through it is bounded on purpose: watch, acknowledge alarms, change authorized setpoints. Reprogramming the controller during a shift belongs to the [[O59rx1hI|engineering station]], not to the HMI.
- The HMI shows the present. History, contextualization and analysis are other layers ([[OShEuklQ|historian]], [[lrh44ra6|MES]]).
