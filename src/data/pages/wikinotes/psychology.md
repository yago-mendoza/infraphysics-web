Psychology studies behaviour and mental processes, including attention, memory, motivation and learning. Explanations can concern different levels: a reported experience, a behavioural pattern or a proposed underlying mechanism. Evidence at one level does not automatically establish the others.

The essay [Working with AI is addictive](/blog/essays/working-with-ai-is-addictive) uses personal experience to examine repeated tool use and reward. That is a starting point for questions about behaviour, rather than a measurement of a biological mechanism. [[rttI47hN|Systems theory]] supplies a language for feedback without resolving those empirical questions.
