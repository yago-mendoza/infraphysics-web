The interpretability holy grail: one neuron, one meaning: no double shifts, no moonlighting, just clean signal. The opposite of [[Sp2tK6jL|superposition]].
- "Towards Monosemanticity: Decomposing Language Models with Dictionary Learning" (Anthropic, 2023), the landmark paper.
- Uses [[Sa4vB8mQ|sparse autoencoders]] to decompose activations into a larger set of interpretable [[Ft9pL5hS|features]]
- The insight: the model DOES have clean features. They're just encoded as directions in superposed space, not as individual neurons.
- Key goal of [[UHGnehtS|mechanistic interpretability]]: if we can extract monosemantic features, we can understand what the model actually knows.
