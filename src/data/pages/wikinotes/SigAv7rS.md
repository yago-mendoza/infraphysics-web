Signature-based antivirus identifies malicious files or patterns by comparing them with known indicators. It is fast and precise for threats already catalogued, which is exactly why it remains useful.

Its blind spot is novelty and variation. A new payload or small transformation may avoid a byte-level signature even when its behavior is familiar. Modern endpoint protection therefore combines signatures with reputation, heuristics, telemetry and behavioral detection.

A wanted poster works wonderfully for the face printed on it. It is not a theory of crime.
