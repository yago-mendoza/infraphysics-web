A large plant is not a naive customer. Purchasing is backed by production, maintenance and automation engineers with very high technical knowledge, so the [[Xu7qzqlL|vendor]] or [[cb9f4huK|integrator]] negotiates against people who know what things really cost.
- What they know: the real cost of the hardware, the cost of changing vendor, how long equipment lasts, what a stoppage costs per hour, which spares are critical, which maintenance contracts exist, which systems could be replaced by whom.
- That knowledge is what makes industrial negotiations hard, and it is the buyer's defense against the lock-in the [[o0FC5b14|aftermarket]] is built on. The three departments pull in different directions: purchasing wants price, production wants uptime, maintenance wants spares and support, and the [[o6N4rqGz|ERP]] is where the three meet ([[nLtOMdEo|industrial ecosystem]]).
