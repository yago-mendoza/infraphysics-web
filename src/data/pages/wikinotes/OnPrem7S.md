On-premises infrastructure is hardware and software operated within facilities controlled by the organization rather than consumed entirely from an external cloud provider.

It can improve physical control, predictable utilization economics and compliance options, but transfers capacity planning, maintenance, power, cooling and lifecycle responsibility to the operator. “Own the server” is not the same as “own every dependency”; chips, firmware, models and support chains may remain external.

On-prem buys control by accepting chores the cloud had hidden on the invoice.
