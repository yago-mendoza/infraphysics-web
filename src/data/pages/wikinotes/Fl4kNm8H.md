Cloud platform for running [[Bk9sTm2J|backend]] containers close to users. Similar to [[Rw6pDx3L|Railway]] but with a focus on edge deployment: your containers run in datacenters near your users.
- Persistent processes: runs [[FlMHYCjo|Docker]] containers or VMs, not [[Jn4xWp7B|serverless]] functions
- Multi-region: deploy once, run in multiple [[Pp6rDx2H|PoPs]]. Closer to [[Tm6yRs2K|edge computing]] than Railway, but with full server capabilities
- Good for: real-time apps, game servers, databases, anything that needs a persistent process close to users
- Less opinionated than [[Vc3pLx8B|Vercel]], more infra-flexible than [[Rw6pDx3L|Railway]]
