Methods that find structure in data without labels: directions of variation, groups, and low-dimensional representations.
- [[0WlrLnFU|PCA]] keeps the directions of largest variance. [[9ELAQ3ap|K-means]] groups points around centroids. [[iRDhDtGq|spectral clustering]] first represents connectivity between samples, then groups.
- Their mathematical foundations live under [[4Xrmzz1Y|Math]]: the [[m5zFVv4g|covariance matrix]], [[Vp8NyXtD|projection]] and [[5Gsj9whS|spectral coordinates]].
