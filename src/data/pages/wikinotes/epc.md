**Engineering, Procurement and Construction**: the company or consortium that designs, buys, builds and commissions a large industrial installation (a refinery, a chemical plant, a factory) and hands it over to the owner or operator.
- The EPC is not the plant. In a big project it may buy Siemens, Emerson or ABB systems, subcontract [[cb9f4huK|integrators]], receive [[pC4MKpUX|OEM]] packages, and deliver the whole. Its financial and execution risk is high because the projects are large and long.
- Commercially it sits at the top of one of the possible chains: plant owner, then EPC, then vendor, integrator and OEM. But the owner can also buy from the vendor directly, or through an integrator; there is no single chain ([[nLtOMdEo|industrial ecosystem]]).
- Once the EPC leaves, the relationships that last are the ones with whoever maintains the systems, which is where the [[o0FC5b14|aftermarket]] begins.
