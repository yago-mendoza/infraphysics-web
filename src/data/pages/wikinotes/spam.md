Content or behavior designed mainly to manipulate search, ranking or agents rather than to provide real value. Trying to fool the referee instead of playing better.
- Classic forms: keyword stuffing, hidden text, link farms, cloaking (serving one page to crawlers and another to people). The AI-era form is the same move aimed at models: text invisible to humans written only to steer an agent or an answer engine.
- The line that matters is honesty of audience. A page written explicitly for machines and declared as such, a [[Lm8xKr3P|llms.txt]] or a crawler-only text version served openly, is legitimate; the same words hidden from humans to manipulate an agent look much more like this category.
- Engines fight it with [[PTYwZyrc|ranking]] penalties and manual actions, and training-data pipelines filter it out of crawls ([[Xe6SbANI|Common Crawl]]).
