Quantum-inspired algorithms are classical algorithms that borrow mathematical structures or intuitions developed in quantum information and physics. They still run on ordinary classical hardware unless explicitly implemented on a quantum computer.

The label can describe real technical lineage, such as tensor-network representations, but it is also unusually vulnerable to marketing fog. The grounding question is concrete: which mathematical object was borrowed, what approximation does it enable, and what hardware executes it?

“Inspired by flight” does not make a train an aircraft. Inspiration names ancestry, not substrate.
