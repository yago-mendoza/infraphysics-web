Physics describes how physical systems change under constraints such as conservation of mass and energy. A useful model selects variables and assumptions that preserve the behaviour relevant to a question, while leaving other details out. Its predictions depend on those choices.

For the tank and drone models in this notebook, the route into the equations is through [[Vsap2qco|balance equations]] and [[AjNT7Eno|differential equations]]. A numerical result is only as physical as the assumptions and units behind it.
