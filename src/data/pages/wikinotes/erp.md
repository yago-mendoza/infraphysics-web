**Enterprise Resource Planning**, the corporate level of the [[WY7g1n8W|automation pyramid]]: purchasing, sales, inventory, logistics, finance, human resources and business planning. Examples: SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics.
- The ERP decides what to manufacture; it does not know how a valve is opened. Execution belongs to the [[lrh44ra6|MES]], control to the [[mfLejTTj|PLC]] and [[7NrcWZUv|DCS]].
- Its purchasing module is one of the actors of the plant's [[nLtOMdEo|ecosystem]]: it is where maintenance contracts, spares and upgrades are bought, and where the [[o0FC5b14|aftermarket]] shows up as a line item for decades.
