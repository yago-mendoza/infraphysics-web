Knowledge management makes recorded information retrievable and usable beyond the moment it was captured. It requires decisions about what a note means, where it came from and how it relates to other notes. Accumulating text alone does not make those relationships recoverable.

A personal second brain is one implementation of this idea. This site uses stable concept identifiers and explicit links, with the [[BoSed9wO|content pipeline]] turning source notes into navigable pages. Search retrieves matching material; links preserve relationships the author has chosen to state.
