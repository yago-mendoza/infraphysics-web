Concrete standards, one note each: the link layer ([[aHC0Nhas|Ethernet]], [[PrYQJgli|RS-485]]), the Internet stack ([[d0JwXdPu|TCP/IP]], [[g9jb8QD1|HTTPS]]), messaging ([[9y91dC7S|MQTT]]) and the automotive and embedded bus ([[giue07uO|CAN]]).
- The concepts they share are under [[kmV0rkWB|network theory]]. Standards that only make sense inside a plant (Modbus, PROFINET, EtherCAT, OPC UA) are under [[ctpMShFs|fieldbus]], [[1pkzK8nT|industrial Ethernet]] and [[KApGpKIt|data]].
