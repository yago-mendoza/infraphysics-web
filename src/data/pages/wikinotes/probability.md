The mathematics of uncertainty: distributions over outcomes, how they propagate, and what they converge to.
- A [[HF547EI2|Markov chain]] propagates a distribution over a finite set of states one step at a time; its long run is the [[4lWVJGgV|stationary distribution]].
- [[StatP0cc|statistics]] is the other direction: reasoning back from observed outcomes to the process that produced them.
