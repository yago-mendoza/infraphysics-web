The industrial Ethernet of the Mitsubishi Electric ecosystem, successor of the serial CC-Link fieldbus, widespread in Japanese and Asian machinery. It offers gigabit bandwidth with deterministic cyclic exchange for control.
- It belongs to the same story as PROFINET and EtherNet/IP: a large vendor with its own PLCs, drives and robots, its own control network, and integrators who speak it ([[1pkzK8nT|industrial Ethernet]], [[Xu7qzqlL|automation vendor]]). A plant that buys a Mitsubishi packaging line and a Siemens process line ends up bridging the two.
