- The practice and infrastructure of connecting computing devices so they can exchange data.
- Encompasses physical media (copper, fiber, wireless), addressing schemes, and protocol stacks.
- Relies on packet switching and layered abstractions: from Ethernet frames to TCP segments to HTTP requests.
- [[Q1kGheNp|cache]] (CDN, proxy) reduces latency by serving repeated content from the edge instead of the origin.
- Concepts that hold for any network are under [[kmV0rkWB|network theory]] (protocol stack, determinism, publish-subscribe); concrete standards under [[iEQTD3F0|network protocols]] (Ethernet, TCP/IP, HTTPS, MQTT, CAN, RS-485).
