Data residency describes the geographic location where data is stored or processed. It matters for contractual commitments, sector rules, latency, incident handling and exposure to legal jurisdictions.

Residency is narrower than privacy and much narrower than technological sovereignty. Data can remain inside one country while encryption keys, software updates, support access and the controlling company sit elsewhere. A region selector answers one coordinate, not the entire governance question.

Knowing where the warehouse stands does not tell you who owns the keys.
