The company that joins equipment of different manufacturers into one working system: a Siemens PLC with ABB drives, Endress+Hauser sensors and an Ignition SCADA. It programs, configures communications, builds HMI and SCADA screens and carries out commissioning.
- Its knowledge is precisely the seams: which protocol each box speaks, which [[DML5M3qP|register map]] the meter uses, how a KUKA [[hgytSVXF|robot controller]] handshakes with a Rockwell PLC ([[1pkzK8nT|industrial Ethernet]]).
- An integrator does not necessarily program the SCADA and disappear. It can design the automation, commission it, and then stay five, ten or more years under a **maintenance contract** for PLCs, DCS, SCADA, networks, program changes, migrations, new machines, spares and 24/7 support. That turns an initial hardware sale into a very long [[o0FC5b14|aftermarket]] relationship.
- It buys from [[Xu7qzqlL|vendors]], works for plants, [[HHIC3rhB|EPCs]] and [[pC4MKpUX|OEMs]], and negotiates with a [[rNOGsjVE|sophisticated buyer]] that knows what its hours are worth.
