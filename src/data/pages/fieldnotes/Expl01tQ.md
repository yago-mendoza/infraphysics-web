An exploit is a technique, input or program that takes advantage of a vulnerability to produce an unintended effect. The vulnerability is the weakness; the exploit is the operational recipe.

Working exploitation often requires more than discovering the flaw. Environmental details, mitigations, timing, permissions and target versions determine whether the method is reliable. A proof of concept demonstrates possibility. A robust exploit turns possibility into a repeatable capability.

Finding the crack and fitting a lever into it are different engineering tasks.
