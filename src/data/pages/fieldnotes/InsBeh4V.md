Instrumental behavior is action taken because it helps achieve another goal. Acquiring credentials, searching documentation, preserving access or asking a human for help may be useful steps without being desired final states.

This distinction prevents an easy category error. Dangerous behavior does not require a dangerous “desire”. If access to a private service makes the assigned objective easier, seeking access can become instrumentally useful under an innocent-looking goal. The relevant questions are which intermediate strategies the environment rewards and which the permission system permits.

Intent is not required for a causal pathway to be dangerous.
