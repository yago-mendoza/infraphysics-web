A closed model is accessed through a product or API while its trained weights and much of its implementation remain controlled by the provider. Customers buy capability and service rather than possession of the model artifact.

This can provide rapid upgrades, strong managed infrastructure and low operational burden. It also limits independent modification, auditing and portability. The provider can change prices, policies, behavior or availability, so integration design should treat the model endpoint as an external dependency rather than a permanent law of nature.

Convenience is real. So is the landlord.
