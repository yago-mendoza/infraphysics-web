Cutting a candidate feature set down to the few that carry signal, before trusting an evaluation.
- In rare-event problems it is debt control: every extra feature is a loan against evidence you probably do not have. It fights the [[Cx7mT5nQ|curse of dimensionality]].
- Redundant features (two ways of saying the same thing) get dropped by correlation and by meaning: keep one reading, drop synonyms.
- Fewer features means a model small enough that a human can still argue with it, and less room for [[obTC4dWy|overfitting]].
