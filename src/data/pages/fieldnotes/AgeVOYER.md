- [[d6uchFbH|Instruction Following Evaluation]] tests precise formatting, length, keyword, and structural constraint compliance using prompts with verifiable instructions.
- Not about knowledge or reasoning, purely about doing exactly what was asked.
- Its strength is also its boundary: deterministic checks make scoring reproducible, but passing the check does not prove that the response is useful.

[[EvalP0aa|evaluation]] : : IFEval isolates instruction compliance from broad capability and style judgments
