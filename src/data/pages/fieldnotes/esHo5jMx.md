Take a model that read the entire internet and teach it to do one thing properly. Take a [[2oNdlB5L|pre-trained]] model, train further on task-specific data.
- Full fine-tuning updates all weights — expensive, risks catastrophic forgetting.
- [[3kgsj4Y4|LoRA]] and adapters make it cheaper: freeze most weights, train a small delta.
- The bridge between general knowledge and specific skill.
