Amazon Web Services. The original cloud platform, the "default" when someone says "cloud hosting."
- Massive catalog: compute (EC2), storage ([[S3m8Kw4R|S3]]), serverless ([[Lb5nCx3G|Lambda]]), databases (RDS, DynamoDB), ML (SageMaker, Bedrock)
- More complex and enterprise-oriented than [[Hp5nVw9C|Cloudflare]] or [[Vc3pLx8B|Vercel]]
- [[Vc3pLx8B|Vercel]] runs on AWS under the hood: Vercel Functions are [[Lb5nCx3G|Lambda]], Vercel's CDN sits on top of AWS infrastructure
