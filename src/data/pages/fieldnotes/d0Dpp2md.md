DeepMind, 2016. Beat Lee Sedol at Go — the game everyone said AI couldn't play for decades. Monte Carlo tree search + deep neural nets for position evaluation + policy network for move selection. AlphaGo Zero then learned from scratch with zero human games, surpassing the original. The moment AI became visceral for the public.
