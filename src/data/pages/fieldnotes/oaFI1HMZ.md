Google is simultaneously a consumer product company, an advertising market, a cloud provider, and one of the largest collections of developer platforms. Treating “Google” as one tool hides where the operational boundaries actually are.

- [[Ng9LJhY5|Cloud Console]] owns projects, billing, credentials, quotas, and cloud resources.
- [[Qc71eVI9|Admin Console]] governs an organization's Workspace users and policies.
- [[DkJNApLt|Search Console]] describes how Google Search sees a site; [[U99MuNPb|Analytics]] describes instrumented user activity on it.
- [[vf6KPYwT|Google APIs]] expose individual products such as [[2DTZTKbQ|Gmail]] and Maps to software.

The recurring trap is identity: a Google account, Cloud project, OAuth consent screen, service account, Workspace organization, and billing account can all belong to the same human while remaining different security principals.
