Open-source, community-maintained map database. The Wikipedia of maps — anyone can edit, and the data is free to use.
- Used by Mapbox, Leaflet, Apple Maps (partially), Meta, Microsoft, and thousands of apps
- No API fees for the raw data (unlike [[CVqN8DmF|Google Maps Platform]])
- Rendering and routing require your own infrastructure or third-party services (Mapbox, OSRM, Nominatim)
- Data quality varies by region — excellent in Europe, uneven in developing countries
