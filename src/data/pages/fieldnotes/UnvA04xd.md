A [[YrmsQuhU|neural network]] with even one hidden layer can approximate any continuous function to arbitrary precision, given enough neurons (Cybenko 1989, Hornik 1991).
- An existence proof, not a recipe: it says a good weight setting exists, not how to find it, nor how many neurons "enough" is.
- Depth, in practice, is what makes the search tractable, not raw width.
- Explains why nets are expressive, but not why training via [[aufHHy2p|backpropagation]] actually finds good solutions.
