- Search Engine Optimization — making web content discoverable and rankable by search engines.
- Core pillars: crawlability (can engines find your pages), indexability (will they store them), and relevance (will they rank them).
- Technical SEO: [[Cx7nWr4L|robots.txt]], [[Sm9pLx3R|sitemap]], canonical URLs, structured data, page speed, mobile-friendliness.
- On-page SEO: title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, internal linking, content quality.
- The game changed with AI — [[Ax4kJn8M|AEO]] is now a parallel discipline. Search engines still matter, but LLMs are becoming a primary way people find information.
- Key distinction: SEO optimizes for ranking in a list. AEO optimizes for being cited in an answer.
