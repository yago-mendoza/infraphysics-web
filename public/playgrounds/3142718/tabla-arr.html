ARR2 / Tank 2 balance

Checks M3; uses M3, M4, M6, M7, M9, M10, M13.

r3 = mUp − PI(h1c − my1)

ARR4 / Pump consistency

r4 = mQp − f(mUp)

Checks M5; uses M5, M11, M12.

Q̂12 = f(my1, my2, mUb)

Q̂o = f(my2, Uo)

Each f is the corresponding component law in M5–M7. M5 includes the pump's piecewise law; its linear gain alone is insufficient at saturation. PI denotes the implemented controller, with matching timing, initial state and limiting logic.

The two hydraulic residuals

ARR1 and ARR2 share M6, M9, M10 and M13: the inter-tank valve model and the three measurements used to evaluate it.

+Q̂12 in r1; −Q̂12 in r2. Same dependency, opposite sign.

ARR1 uses the measured pump flow through M11, not the pump model M5. ARR4 is the residual that checks M5.
