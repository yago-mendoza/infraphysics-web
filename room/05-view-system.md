# Global view system

The redesign must be coherent across the whole site, not only on Home.

## Persistent shell

### Desktop navbar

The navbar should be quiet, wide, and always legible. It should identify the site, expose the few primary destinations, and hold language/theme/search controls without becoming a product toolbar.

Primary destinations remain intentionally few:

`Home · Writing · Projects · About · Contact`

Archive / Second Brain can be present as a secondary destination, but should not dominate the public identity.

### Mobile navbar

Mobile should not be a compressed desktop sidebar. It should become a small identity bar plus a simple full-screen or bottom-sheet navigation surface with the same typography and signal language.

### Footer

The footer should be a short closing note, not a second sitemap. It should contain:

- a concise identity or availability line;
- contact route;
- selected social links;
- language control if it does not belong in the navbar;
- a small InfraPhysics / TRACE colophon.

## View matrix

### Home

Purpose: immediate identity, emotional signal, field of view, and entry into the work.

Should contain: face, name, one strong statement, system trace, field of view, selected work, one contact invitation.

Should not contain: Second Brain statistics, graph controls, lengthy explanations, dashboard panels, or a complete taxonomy.

### Writing

Purpose: show the durable stream of thought.

Should contain: title, short premise, date, visual type marker, series where relevant, list of pieces.

Should not contain: card grids by default, excessive filters, technical metadata before the writing, or category chrome that overwhelms the titles.

### Article

Purpose: reading and visual reasoning.

Should contain: type, title, date, series context, readable body, diagrams/images, one useful next path.

Should not contain: persistent application controls, unrelated stats, decorative graph widgets, or a forced related-content wall.

### Projects

Purpose: show concrete making and engineering judgment.

Should contain: question, constraint, intervention, artefact, state, and evidence.

Should not contain: generic portfolio language, glowing timelines, or technology badges as the main visual.

### About

Purpose: explain the person behind the range.

Should contain: identity, TRACE, method, motivations, domains, and a short collaboration-oriented reading.

Should not contain: a conventional CV dump or a list of disconnected skills.

### Contact

Purpose: make an interesting conversation easy.

Should contain: what kinds of problems/ideas are welcome, direct routes, social links, and an optional form.

Should not contain: generic “let's work together” copy with no point of view.

### Archive / Second Brain

Purpose: deeper discovery for visitors who want to follow the structure underneath the work.

Should contain: search, browsing, readable notes, and useful connections.

Should not contain: public-facing editor controls, 3D spectacle, purple dashboard styling, or an obligation to understand the internal data model.

## TRACE integration

TRACE must shape the language of the system, the visual motifs, the way sections are named, and the way the author is presented. It should not be bolted on as a manifesto paragraph after the UI has already been designed. The actual TRACE material must be read before finalizing About, the home statement, and the long-term content model.
